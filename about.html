Our story

From a 10-foot counter to 50,000+ customers

Artificial Vision began in 2018 as a small counter at Nehru Place, New Delhi, one of India's busiest electronics markets. Our founders, two engineers, kept meeting customers who had bought the wrong device because nobody explained the specifications honestly.

So we made a simple promise: test every product ourselves, explain it in plain language and recommend only what fits the customer's needs and budget. That promise took us online in 2020 and led to our own AV range of laptops, phones, audio and wearables in 2022.

Today we serve customers in more than 500 cities, but every order still gets the same care as that first sale at the counter.
